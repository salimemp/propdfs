## 1. Introduction

ProPDFs ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you use our website, mobile applications, and document processing services (collectively, the "Services").

By using ProPDFs, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use our Services.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information You Provide
We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:
- Create an account (email address, password, full name)
- Update your profile (avatar, display name)
- Subscribe to a plan (billing information, payment details via Stripe)
- Contact our support team (support tickets, chat messages)
- Participate in the beta program (feedback, usage data)
- Use our OAuth login options (Google, GitHub - we receive email, name, and profile picture)

### 2.2 Documents and Content
We collect and process documents you upload for conversion, editing, or other PDF operations. This includes:
- PDF files, Word documents, Excel files, PowerPoint presentations, images, and other supported formats
- File metadata (filename, file size, page count, creation date)
- Processing parameters (merge order, split ranges, compression settings, etc.)

**Important:** Documents are processed to fulfill your requests and are automatically deleted after 24 hours unless you explicitly save them to your account storage. We do not access the content of your documents for any purpose other than processing your requested operations.

### 2.3 Usage Information
We automatically collect information about how you interact with our Services:
- IP address and device information (browser type, operating system, device type)
- Log data (access times, pages visited, features used)
- Usage statistics (number of conversions, tools used, processing time)
- Error reports and crash logs
- Referral information (how you found our service)

### 2.4 AI Processing Data
When you use our AI features (summarization, translation, chat), we:
- Send document content to Google Gemini API for processing
- Do not store AI processing results unless you explicitly save them
- Do not use your documents to train AI models
- Process AI requests through encrypted connections

### 2.5 OCR Processing Data
When you use OCR features:
- Images and scanned documents are processed using Google ML Kit (mobile) or Tesseract (backend)
- Extracted text is returned to you and not stored permanently
- Processing occurs on our secure servers or your device (for mobile OCR)

## 6. Data Storage and Security

### 6.1 Storage Location
- **Primary storage:** United States (Cloudflare R2, PostgreSQL on Railway)
- **Backup storage:** Encrypted backups in multiple geographic regions
- **AI processing:** United States (Google Gemini API)

### 6.2 Security Measures
We implement industry-standard security measures:
- **Encryption at rest:** All stored files and database content encrypted with AES-256
- **Encryption in transit:** TLS 1.3 for all data transfers
- **Authentication:** JWT tokens with secure, httpOnly cookies
- **Access controls:** Role-based access, least-privilege principles
- **Monitoring:** Continuous security monitoring and intrusion detection
- **SOC 2 compliance:** We follow SOC 2 Type II security controls
- **Regular audits:** Annual security audits and penetration testing

### 6.3 Data Retention
- **Account information:** Retained until you delete your account
- **Documents:** Automatically deleted 24 hours after processing unless saved to your account
- **Saved documents:** Retained until you delete them or your account
- **Usage logs:** Retained for 90 days for security and troubleshooting
- **Payment records:** Retained for 7 years per tax regulations
- **Deleted accounts:** All personal data purged within 30 days of deletion request

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

### 7.1 GDPR Rights (EU/EEA Users)
If you are in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the right to:
- **Access:** Request a copy of your personal data
- **Rectification:** Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erasure (Right to be Forgotten):** Request deletion of your data
- **Restriction:** Request limited processing of your data
- **Data Portability:** Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Objection:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw consent at any time
- **Lodge a Complaint:** File a complaint with your local data protection authority

### 7.2 CCPA Rights (California Users)
If you are a California resident, you have the right to:
- **Know:** Request disclosure of categories and specific pieces of personal information collected
- **Delete:** Request deletion of personal information (with certain exceptions)
- **Opt-Out:** Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data)
- **Non-Discrimination:** Not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights

### 7.3 Other Regional Rights
- **UK GDPR:** Same rights as EU GDPR post-Brexit
- **PIPEDA (Canada):** Right to access and challenge accuracy
- **LGPD (Brazil):** Similar rights to GDPR
- **APPI (Japan):** Right to disclosure, correction, deletion

### 7.4 How to Exercise Your Rights
To exercise any of these rights:
- Email: privacy@propdfs.com
- Use the "Data Privacy" section in your account settings
- Submit a request through our data deletion page: /delete-account

We will respond within 30 days (or sooner as required by local law). We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.
